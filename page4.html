Basic hair care!

Basic hair care comes down to keeping your scalp clean, your hair moisturized, and treating it gently.

Wash your hair appropriately

Find your groove: Many people do well washing every 2-4 days, but it can vary. Use lukewarm water: Hot water can increase dryness and irritation. Focus on the scalp: Shampoo cleans the roots; you can let foam rinse through the lengths. Condition the lengths: Conditioner is mainly for the hair shaft, not the scalp (unless your scalp is very dry and it suits you).

Right products

Shampoo: Use a gentle, scalp-friendly formula; consider clarifying shampoo occasionally if buildup is an issue. Conditioner: Pick one that matches your hair type (straight/wavy/curly, fine/thick) and aims at softness/less frizz. Tweak for your needs: If you get flakes or itching, use an anti-dandruff shampoo as directed (and give it time).

Dry and detangle carefully

Blot, don't rub: A microfiber towel or soft T-shirt reduces breakage. Detangle when needed: Use a wide-tooth comb or brush appropriate for your hair texture. Start from the ends: Work upward gradually to avoid tugging

Protect hair from damage

Limit heat: If you use tools, apply a heat protectant and keep temperatures reasonable. Reduce friction: Satin/silk pillowcases and hair-friendly hair ties help prevent breakage. Be gentle with styling: Avoid tight hairstyles that pull at the hairline.

Maintain good habits

And last but certainly not least Trim regularly: Small trims (try every few months) help prevent split ends from traveling. Watch for signs: Excessive shedding, persistent scalp irritation, or sudden major changes may mean it’s worth seeing a dermatologist.
