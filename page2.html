Our story

How we came to be.

We first came up with the idea for our company when me and my partner were only rookie stylists. After a long day of work me and her would have dinner at our local diner and we would say "what would you do if we had our own place?" At first we would laugh at it like it was an impossible dream but as the years had gone by we started to actually think about the possiblity of it becoming a reality. We started studying business as well as getting our degrees in business and how to run an establishment. As we got our degrees, we then started to form a plan. We wrote everything down of how we would run things and how we would manage a place while also practicing our craft. The first thing we had to do is find a base of operations. We looked for 3 months online and checked with a few agents to help us find a good establishment. We did not find a good fit at first, some of the places we had visited were not managable for a salon. some were too pricy, some had a lot of structual damage, and some were just not to our liking. It was at the three month mark until we found the building we are currently in now. As soon as we stepped inside, we fell in love. We could see clear as day that this would be our home. of courrse like every building it needed some work but it was doable. We painted the walls, added the new tile. and got a lot of repais done. When that was all accomplished, we started to buy our furniture and equipment. Our dream was becoming true, we just needed one last thing. We needed stylists to work at our company. We have hired many talented people since we have opened and each and every one of our stylists are amazing in their craft. Me and my partner could not be more grateful for our wonderful staff.

What is our goal?

Our goal for this business is to help as many people become thier best selves and to want to express themselves in their best way. We beleive that with help and advice, everyone can look their best selves and be confident with what is on their heads. We want people to love themselves and look the best for others to so that love and happiness can flourish in what we do as an art and as a service to people in our big community.
