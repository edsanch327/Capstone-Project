Damaged hair

What causes your hair to go bad? Well... The answer might surprise you.

Heat

Blow-drying on high heat Flat ironing/curling with no heat protectant Repeated passes over the same section

Chemical treatments

Bleaching/lightening (especially repeated) Relaxers/perm solutions Hair dye (especially frequent high-lift or multiple processes) Frequent toning/straightening treatments

Friction and physical stress

Rough towel drying (rubbing instead of blotting) Tight hairstyles (traction from braids, buns, ponytails) Aggressive brushing or detangling when dry Wearing extensions/wigs that tug on hair

Over-washing or harsh washing habits(Don't be mean to your hair)

Shampooing too frequently for your hair type Using very stripping cleansers Not conditioning after washing (or using conditioner that doesn't suit your hair)

Environmental exposure

Sun/UV damage (can dry and weaken hair) Chlorine (swimming pools) and saltwater Dry air/cold weather stripping moisture

Poor moisture/protein balance

Not enough conditioning can leave hair brittle Too much protein (or the wrong "repair" products) can make hair feel stiff or rough

Your hair is a very delicate thing. It needs care. And you have to be the one to take care of it!
