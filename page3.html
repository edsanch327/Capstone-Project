Hair Dictionary!

Blonde

Blonde hair is a human hair color marked by low amounts of the dark pigment eumelanin, which results in yellowish or golden shades.

Brown

Brown hair is the second most common hair color globally after black hair. It is a defined by moderate levels of the dark pigment eumelanin and low levels of pheomelanin.

Black

Black hair is the darkest and the most common human hair color globally, defined biologically by a high concentratrion of eumelanin and the absense of pheomelanin. The term encompasses both natural hair color across various populations.

Styles

Crew Cut

A crew cut is a short style with the sides and back trimmed close while the top stays slightly longer. It's typically easy to maintain and works well for many hair textures. Styling usually just needs minimal product and a quick comb or brush.

Pompadour

A pompadour features longer hair on top that's styled upward and back for height. It often uses some wax or pomade to hold the shape and can be tailored from subtle volume to a dramatic look. It generally requires more regular styling than very short cuts.

Layers

Layering removes bulk and adds movement, especially for shoulder-length hair. The layers can be cut to frame the face and create a smoother, more textured shape. It usually looks best with light styling like blow-drying or a bit of mousse to enhance the volume and flow
